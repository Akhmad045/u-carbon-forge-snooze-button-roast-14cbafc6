One line, under 200 characters, roasting an alarm clock that rings at
6 AM on a Saturday morning — written for the
[Snooze Button Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

> My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on a Saturday with a court order: my dreams were evicted and I was sentenced to consciousness.

A setup, a turn, and a payoff in the last few words: the clock doesn't
just ring, it serves legal papers — the dreams get evicted and waking
up becomes a sentence with no appeal.

## How it works

`main.py` prints that single line and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` and nothing else. It validates the line against
the competition gate first (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, single line, no
leading or trailing white space).

```sh
python3 main.py                 # prints the line, writes /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test     # validates the entry against the gate
```
